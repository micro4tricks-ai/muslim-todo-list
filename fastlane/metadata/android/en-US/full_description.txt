A free Muslim companion for your day, made as sadaqa jariya. No ads, no tracking, open source (MIT).

<b>Prayer</b>
• Prayer times for 32 countries and 12 calculation methods, on a watch-style clock
• Adhan notifications (four recordings, a separate Fajr adhan) and an iqamah reminder
• My prayers: log each prayer, the twelve sunnah rak'ahs, witr and duha, and prayers to make up
• Qibla compass and nearby mosques
• Home-screen widget with a countdown to the next prayer

<b>Quran</b>
• The full Mushaf in the Uthmani script, with a colour-coded tajweed edition
• Recitation from 9 reciters, including al-Husary's teaching recitation with repetition
• 9 tafsirs, word-by-word meanings with pronunciation, translations in 10 languages
• Daily portion, completion plan, bookmarks, search, memorisation test, and Juz 'Amma for children

<b>Knowledge</b>
• The six hadith books and al-Muwatta with gradings (al-Albani and others), plus Riyad as-Salihin, Bulugh al-Maram and more
• The Prophet's prayer step by step, Hajj and Umrah guides, istikhara, the 99 Names

<b>Seasons and reminders</b>
• Hijri calendar with reminders for Monday and Thursday fasts, the White Days, Arafah, Ashura and more, each with its hadith
• Ramadan: suhoor and iftar countdowns, fasting tracker and a shareable imsakiya
• Reminders shown as beautiful cards you can share

<b>Your day</b>
• Tasks arranged around the prayers, a focus timer, habits, notes, review cards and reports
• Zakat calculator, date converter, backup file, dark mode, Arabic and English
