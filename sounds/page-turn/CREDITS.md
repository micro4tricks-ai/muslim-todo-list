# Page-turn sounds

`page-turn-1.wav` and `page-turn-2.wav` are `bookFlip1.ogg` and `bookFlip2.ogg` from
**RPG Audio** by Kenney Vleugels ([kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio)),
licensed [Creative Commons Zero (CC0)](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Converted to mono 22 kHz WAV (so every browser plays them); the sound itself is unchanged.
Used by `js/flipbook.js` when a page of a flip-book Mushaf turns.
