# Adhan recordings

They come from Wikimedia Commons, Freesound and the Internet Archive, each under the licence
shown on its page. They were trimmed of leading/trailing silence (and of the quiet tail after the
last phrase), levelled and re-encoded to 64 kbps mono MP3 for the app; nothing else was changed.

The ten Egyptian reciters' adhans below come from one Internet Archive item, [موسوعة الاذان 90 اذان](https://archive.org/details/90---azan---90---azan--many----sound----mp3---alazan) (uploaded 2014 by «شبكة ربيع الفردوس الاعلى»), one file each. **The item states no licence and the recordings are old Egyptian radio/studio recordings, so their rights are unverified [needs verification]**: the owner chose to include them; remove them if a rights holder objects.

Re-encoded as above (leading silence removed, levelled, 64 kbps mono). The reciter was taken from the track's title in the item; nobody has listened to them to confirm it.

`noon_chime.wav` (the short chime for reminders and alerts) is generated by the project itself: three sine tones, no third-party material.
